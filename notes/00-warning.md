# Read this before unlocking bootloader
> [!WARNING]
> - OnePlus has introduced **Anti-Rollback Protection (ARB)** in OnePlus Nord CE3 5G (ziti) with the update **OOS15.0.0.1301**.
> - Users on this update or ANY newer OxygenOS version are supposed to flash the current OxygenOS version installed in your system on the other slot of their device.

To check your current OxygenOS version follow the below steps:-
1. open Settings
2. Go to "About Phone"
3. Then "Version"
![About Phone Version](../src../about-phone-version.png)
4. Here the current version is OOS15.0.0.1604 i.e. 1604
